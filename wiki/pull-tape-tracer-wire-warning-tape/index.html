Wiki / Shared components

Pull tape, tracer wire and warning tape

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: these cheap items decide whether the next crew can use and find your conduit years from now.

ItemWhat it is for
Pull tape (mule tape)Left inside conduit to pull cable later; often marked in feet
Tracer wireLets locators find non-metallic conduit and fiber from the surface
Warning tapeBuried above the conduit to warn the next excavator

Notes

  • Leave pull tape in every empty or spare conduit, tied off at both ends.
  • Make tracer wire continuous, with access points in pull boxes, and test it.
  • Place warning tape at the depth the specifications or plans show.

These are rarely paid as separate items, so they are easy to forget. They are also easy for an inspector to check.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Spare conduits without pull tape.
  • Tracer wire broken or not connected between boxes.
