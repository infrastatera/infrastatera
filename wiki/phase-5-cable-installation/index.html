Phase 5: Cable installation

Phase 5Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: phase 5 fills the pathway: conductors, fiber and communication cable are pulled from box to box.

Before pulling

Prove the conduit is continuous and clear. A blocked run found during a pull costs a lot more than one found with a mandrel.

What is pulled

  • Power: service conductors, branch circuits and grounding conductors. See Conductors and cables.
  • Fiber optic cable: backbone and distribution fiber, with slack loops left at pull boxes as specified. See Fiber optic communications.
  • Communication and control cable: CAT6, detector lead-in, signal and control cables.

Good practice

  • Don't exceed the cable's pulling tension or bend radius, especially for fiber.
  • Label each cable at both ends and in pull boxes as it is pulled. See Labeling and identification.
  • Keep the reel tags: they hold the cable data for submittals and records.

At the end of this phase, everything is connected physically but nothing is terminated.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Pulling fiber without tension control and damaging it.
  • Not leaving the specified slack in pull boxes.
  • Unlabeled cables in a box with several runs.
