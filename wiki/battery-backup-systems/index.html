Battery backup systems

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: a battery backup system (BBS) keeps a signal or cabinet running for a time when utility power fails.

Main parts

ItemWhat it is for
Inverter and chargerSwitches to battery power and recharges afterward
BatteriesStored energy, sized for the required run time
Transfer switch / bypassLets maintenance bypass the system
Enclosure or cabinet spaceHouses the system, sometimes beside the controller cabinet
AlarmsReport power failure and battery status

How it works

When utility power drops, the system transfers to battery. Depending on the specification, a signal may run normally for a time, then switch to flashing operation to stretch the battery.

Testing

Pull the utility power and confirm the transfer, run time and alarms.

PM notes

Check battery shipping and storage rules, and record the battery install date; batteries have a service life and warranty that start then.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Alarms not wired to the TMC.
  • Batteries stored too long before installation.
