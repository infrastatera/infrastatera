Equipment cabinets

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: the cabinet is the field office of every system: it holds the electronics, protects them from weather and intruders, and is where Caltrans technicians will work.

What goes inside

Controllers (signals, ramp meters), power supplies, surge protection, terminal blocks, switches, routers, patch panels and battery backup, depending on the system. See Traffic signals, Ramp metering and Network equipment: switches, routers, SFPs and cellular.

Types

Caltrans uses standard cabinet models for signals and ramp meters, and specified cabinets for communication hubs and ITS sites. The model is named in the plans or Special Provisions: what they change and why they win. Cabinets are often furnished pre-wired by a manufacturer and may need factory testing before delivery.

PM notes

  • Cabinets are long-lead items. Submit early. See Procurement and long-lead items.
  • Check the approved wiring diagram against the cabinet on delivery.
  • Keep the cabinet wiring prints inside the cabinet at turnover.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Cabinet delivered without the approved wiring diagram.
  • Door alarm not wired back to the TMC.
  • Missing prints in the cabinet at acceptance.
