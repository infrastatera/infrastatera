Glossary

BCIOPRSTW
BMPBest Management Practice
A measure that keeps sediment and pollutants out of storm drains during construction, such as fiber rolls, inlet protection or covering stockpiles.
CCOContract Change Order
A written change to the contract, issued by Caltrans, that adjusts the work, the price or the time.
CCTVClosed-Circuit Television
Traffic cameras installed along the highway so the operations center can watch conditions live.
CMSChangeable Message Sign
An electronic sign over or beside the road that shows messages sent from the operations center.
CPM scheduleCritical Path Method schedule
The project schedule that links activities in sequence and shows which ones control the finish date.
ITSIntelligent Transportation Systems
The field devices and communications that monitor and manage traffic: cameras, detection, message signs, ramp meters and the network that links them.
OTDROptical Time-Domain Reflectometer
An instrument that sends light pulses down a fiber and reads the reflections to locate splices, connectors, breaks and losses along the run.
Pull box
A buried box with a lid, placed along conduit runs, where crews pull, splice and later access cables.
REResident Engineer
The Caltrans engineer in charge of the contract in the field, and the contractor's main point of contact.
RFIRequest for Information
A written question to the engineer when the contract documents are unclear or conflict with each other or with field conditions.
SFPSmall Form-factor Pluggable
A plug-in transceiver that connects a network switch port to fiber or copper cable.
Special Provisions
The part of a contract that changes and adds to the Standard Specifications for that one project.
Standard Specifications
The Caltrans rulebook that applies to every contract: how work is done, which materials are acceptable, and how work is measured and paid.
Submittal
Product data, shop drawings or samples the contractor sends for approval before ordering or installing material.
SWPPPStorm Water Pollution Prevention Plan
The storm water plan required on larger projects, under the state's construction general permit.
TMCTraffic Management Center
The Caltrans district center that monitors and controls field devices such as cameras, detectors and message signs.
WPCPWater Pollution Control Program
The contractor's plan for keeping sediment and pollutants out of storm drains on smaller projects; larger ones need a SWPPP.

For learning only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Caltrans, and not a contract interpretation: always refer to the contract documents for your project.
