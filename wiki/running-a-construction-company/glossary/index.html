Glossary

Aggregate bonding limit
The total amount of uncompleted work a surety will support at once. Read more
Backlog
Contracted work not yet performed. Read more
Bid/no-bid
The decision whether to spend effort bidding a job, ideally made against a checklist. Read more
Break-even volume
The amount of work at which gross profit just covers overhead. Read more
Burden
Payroll costs on top of wages: taxes, workers' compensation and benefits. Read more
Code of Safe Practices
A written set of safety rules for the employer's operations, required for construction in California. Read more
Cost code
A code that groups job costs by activity or bid item so actual cost can be compared with the estimate. Read more
Experience modification
A workers' compensation factor comparing an employer's actual losses with expected losses; 1.00 is average. Read more
Factoring
Selling receivables to a third party at a discount to get cash sooner. Read more
General indemnity agreement
The agreement in which the contractor and usually its owners promise to repay the surety for any losses. Read more
IIPPInjury and Illness Prevention Program
The written safety program every California employer must have and use. Read more
Job costing
Recording every cost against a job and cost code to track actual against estimate. Read more
Line of credit
A bank loan you draw and repay as needed, used for timing gaps in cash. Read more
Margin
Profit as a share of the selling price. Read more
Markup
The amount added to cost to set the price, expressed as a share of cost. Read more
Overbilling
Billing more than the value of work earned to date; a liability on the balance sheet. Read more
Overhead
Costs that keep the company running but don't belong to one job. Read more
Percent complete
How much of a job is done, measured from the field or from cost to estimated total cost. Read more
Prequalification
An owner or GC's review of a contractor's finances, safety, experience and legal history before allowing it to bid. Read more
Project labor agreement
A collective bargaining agreement covering one project that all contractors on it must follow. Read more
Qualifier
The person whose experience and exams qualify a contractor license. Read more
RMEResponsible managing employee
An employee who qualifies a company's contractor license. Read more
RMOResponsible managing officer
An officer of a corporation who qualifies its contractor license. Read more
Single job bonding limit
The largest single contract a surety will bond for a contractor. Read more
Tailgate meeting
A short job site safety meeting led by a supervisor; required at least every 10 working days in California construction. Read more
Underbilling
Work earned but not yet billed; an asset that can signal unsigned changes or a job in trouble. Read more
WIP scheduleWork-in-progress schedule
A list of open jobs with contract value, estimated cost, cost to date, percent complete and over or under billing. Read more
Working capital
Current assets minus current liabilities; the base sureties and banks use to size capacity. Read more

For learning only, not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice. Laws, amounts and deadlines change: check the official sources linked in each article, and talk to your CPA, attorney, surety agent or insurance broker before you decide.
