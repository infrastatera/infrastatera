Building relationships with GCs, agencies and suppliers

California, November 2026Last verified November 25, 2026

In one sentence: repeat work comes from people who trust you to show up, tell the truth early and finish clean, so every job is also a sales call.

With general contractors

  • Submit complete bids on time, with clear scope and exclusions.
  • Hit submittal and schedule dates; GCs remember who held them up.
  • Tell them about problems early, with a proposed solution.
  • Clean closeout: punch list, as-builts, O&M manuals without chasing.

With agencies and inspectors

  • Professional, complete paperwork: daily reports, certified payroll, submittals.
  • Respect for the inspector's role, even in disagreement.
  • Preconstruction and progress meetings: be prepared, short and factual.

With suppliers

  • Pay on time; a supplier who trusts you gives better prices, terms and allocation in a shortage.
  • Share bid schedules early so they can price.
  • Give them feedback on their quotes after the bid.

Between jobs

  • After each job, ask the PM or owner what went well and what didn't.
  • Attend industry associations and agency outreach events.
  • Keep a short list of key contacts and talk to each a few times a year.

PM notes

Reputation is local and fast. One unpaid supplier or one abandoned punch list travels further than ten good jobs.

Common mistakes

  • Only calling customers when there's a bid.
  • Hiding problems until they're claims.
  • Paying suppliers late to fund other jobs.
