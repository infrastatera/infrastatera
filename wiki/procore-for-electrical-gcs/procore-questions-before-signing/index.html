Questions to ask before signing

Based on Procore's public help pages, November 2026Last verified November 25, 2026

In one sentence: the questions that matter most are about the contract and your data, because they're the hardest to change after you sign.

Contract

  • How long is the term, and what notice is needed to cancel or change?
  • How is the price calculated, and how can it change at renewal?
  • Which tools and how many users or projects are included?
  • What does adding a tool later cost?

Your data

  • How do you export all project data and documents, and in which formats? See Keeping your records after the job.
  • What happens to your data at the end of the contract, and for how long can you still get it?
  • Who can see your data, including collaborators you invite?

Support and training

  • What training is included for office and field users?
  • Is there a person assigned to your account, or only general support?
  • How are new features and changes communicated?

Fit for Caltrans electrical work

  • Ask for a demo using your documents: a Caltrans plan set, special provisions, a daily report, an RFI.
  • Check how the Specifications tool handles Caltrans section numbering. See Drawings and specifications in Procore.
  • Check how certified payroll would be produced. See Timesheets, crew hours and certified payroll.
  • Talk to another contractor of your size doing public works who uses it.

Before you decide

Write down the three problems you're buying it to solve (see Is Procore worth it for a small electrical GC?) and check each answer against them.

Common mistakes

  • Signing a multi-year term before a real job has been run.
  • Not asking about price changes at renewal.
  • No written answer on data export.
