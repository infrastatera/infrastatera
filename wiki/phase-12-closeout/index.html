Phase 12: Project closeout and delivery

Phase 12Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: closeout turns a finished system into an accepted contract and the final payment.

Documentation

As-built plans, material certifications, fiber splice diagrams, test results, warranty packages, equipment manuals and configuration files. See Closeout package checklist.

Administrative

  • Final quantity reconciliation: agree with Caltrans on the measured quantity of every unit-price item.
  • Final schedule update.
  • Final reports and any remaining certified payroll or compliance documents.
  • Change orders all executed. See Change orders and potholing findings.

Turnover

The system is transferred to Caltrans operations and maintenance. After acceptance, the warranty period starts.

PM focus

Start building the closeout package on day one: certificates with submittals, test results as they are run, as-builts as changes happen. Closeout is slow when it starts at the end.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Rebuilding as-builts from memory at the end.
  • Final quantities disputed because the daily records do not support them.
  • Warranty start date not agreed in writing.
