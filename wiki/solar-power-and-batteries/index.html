Solar power and LiFePO4 batteries

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: where there is no practical utility service, solar panels charge batteries that run the site day and night.

Main parts

ItemWhat it is for
Solar panelsGenerate electricity
Panel mountsSupport and tilt the panels on the pole or structure
Charge controllerRegulates battery charging and protects the batteries
Batteries (often LiFePO4)Store energy for night and cloudy days
Battery enclosure and traysHold and protect the batteries
Battery cables and DC disconnectConnect the bank; let crews isolate it safely

Sizing

The design sets panel and battery capacity from the load (camera, radio, router) and the days of autonomy required. Don't substitute smaller equipment.

Batteries

Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries are common for their life and safety, and some contracts require them. They are long-lead items with shipping rules for lithium batteries. See Procurement and long-lead items.

Testing

Caltrans may check charging, battery voltage, and that the site runs on battery alone. See Phase 9: System testing.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Panels facing the wrong direction or shaded.
  • Battery chemistry or capacity different from the approved submittal.
  • No DC disconnect for maintenance.
