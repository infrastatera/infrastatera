Phase 3: Pull boxes and foundations

Phase 3Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: phase 3 creates the access points and the bases that everything above ground will stand on.

Pull boxes

Pull boxes and vaults are set where the conduit runs change direction, at devices, and at intervals along long runs, as shown on the plans. They include communication and fiber pull boxes. See Pull boxes and vaults.

Foundations

Cast-in-place foundations for cabinets, signal and lighting poles, sign structures, camera poles and solar assemblies. Each one needs the right anchor bolts set to template, conduit stubs in the right place and a ground rod or grounding as detailed. See Foundations: cabinet, pole and sign.

Inspection, then concrete

Caltrans inspects the excavation, reinforcement, anchor bolts and conduit before the pour. Concrete must cure before a pole or cabinet is loaded on it. Keep the concrete delivery tickets; they are part of the closeout record.

PM focus

  • Anchor bolt templates and bolts ordered with the poles; they're often needed first.
  • Inspection requested in time for the pour.
  • Correct number and size of conduit stubs: missing one means breaking concrete later.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Pouring before the inspector has seen the foundation.
  • Anchor bolts set to the wrong pattern or orientation.
  • Forgetting a spare conduit stub into a cabinet foundation.
  • Losing concrete tickets.
