Phase 10: Caltrans acceptance testing

Phase 10Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: in phase 10 Caltrans checks that what you built works and is documented, before it takes the system over.

What you provide

Test reports, fiber test results, material certifications, as-builts and warranty information.

What Caltrans does

The Resident Engineer and electrical inspectors witness the tests, sometimes with the district's electrical maintenance or TMC staff, and produce a list of deficiencies: the punch list.

Common findings

  • Labeling issues.
  • Missing documentation.
  • Grounding corrections.
  • Network configuration corrections.

PM focus

Book the witness tests with enough notice, have the crew and any subcontractors on site, and bring the test reports printed. See Caltrans acceptance testing and the punch list.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Scheduling the witness test before the network is fully configured.
  • Missing test equipment calibration certificates.
