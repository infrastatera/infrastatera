Caltrans acceptance testing and the punch list

Not yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: acceptance is when Caltrans confirms the system works and is documented; the punch list is what stands between you and it.

Before the witness test

  • Your own tests are done and passed. See Phase 9: System testing.
  • Test reports are ready in the required format, with calibration certificates.
  • The network is configured and devices report to the TMC.
  • Cabinets are clean, labeled and have prints inside.

During the test

The Resident Engineer and inspectors, sometimes with district electrical or TMC staff, watch the tests and inspect the work. Have the crew, the fiber or network subcontractor, and test equipment on site.

The punch list

Common findings: labeling, missing documentation, grounding corrections, network configuration. Track every item to closure with photos. See Phase 11: Punch list.

Acceptance

Once the punch list is closed and the documents are delivered, Caltrans accepts the work (for a system or the whole contract) and maintenance responsibility passes to Caltrans. See Closeout package checklist.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Scheduling the witness test before your own tests pass.
  • Missing calibration certificates.
  • Punch list items closed without photos.
