Phase 4: Structure installation

Phase 4Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: in phase 4 the job becomes visible: poles stand up and cabinets go on their foundations.

What happens

  • Poles: signal and lighting standards, mast arms, CCTV poles and communication poles, set on cured foundations and plumbed.
  • Solar assemblies: panel mounts and battery enclosures at sites without utility power. See Solar power and LiFePO4 batteries.
  • Equipment cabinets: set on foundations, sealed and grounded. See Equipment cabinets.

PM focus

  • Deliveries in order: poles and cabinets are long-lead items and need storage or same-day setting.
  • Lifting plans and lane closures for setting poles near traffic.
  • Damage on arrival: inspect finishes and record any damage before signing the delivery ticket.
  • Shop drawing match: height, arm length and bolt pattern match the approved submittal.

After this phase, the structures exist, but they aren't wired.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Accepting a damaged pole or cabinet without noting it on the delivery ticket.
  • Setting a pole before the foundation has cured.
  • A cabinet that does not match the approved submittal.
