Changeable message signs

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: a changeable message sign (CMS) shows drivers messages the TMC sends: incidents, travel times, closures.

Main parts

PartWhat it does
Sign housing and displayLED display in a walk-in or front-access housing
Sign structureOverhead or roadside structure and foundation, often by others
Sign controller and cabinetReceives messages and runs the display. See Equipment cabinets
CommunicationFiber or cellular link to the TMC. See Network equipment: switches, routers, SFPs and cellular
PowerService with enough capacity for the display. See Service equipment and utility power

Testing

Caltrans checks every pixel or module, brightness control, and that the TMC can post and clear messages.

PM notes

  • A CMS is a long-lead item and heavy: plan delivery, lifting and lane closures together.
  • Confirm the controller's protocol matches the TMC software at submittal time.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Communication protocol not compatible with the TMC.
  • Setting the sign before the structure is accepted.
