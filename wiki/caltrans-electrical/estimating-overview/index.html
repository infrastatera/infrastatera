How a Caltrans electrical bid comes together

Last verified November 25, 2026

In one sentence: a Caltrans electrical bid is a few weeks of work squeezed between the ad and the bid opening: read the package, take off quantities, price labor, material, equipment and subs, check it, and submit it electronically.

The steps

  1. Decide whether to bid. Scope, location, size, schedule, risk, your workload. See Saying no: which jobs to pass on.
  2. Get the package: plans, special provisions, Bid Item List, addenda. See The bid package: bid items, quantities and the Bid Item List.
  3. Read it and list questions and risks. See Reading the Bid Item List.
  4. Take off quantities from the plans. See Electrical quantity takeoff.
  5. Price labor from crew productivity. See Pricing labor: crews and production rates.
  6. Get material quotes. See Material pricing and supplier quotes.
  7. Price equipment and indirect costs. See Equipment, traffic control and indirect costs.
  8. Collect subcontractor quotes and plan bid day. See Subcontractor quotes and bid day.
  9. Check the bid before it goes. See Checking your bid before it goes.
  10. Submit electronically before the deadline, then learn from the results. See After the bid: results, award and lessons.

How much time you have

Caltrans says its projects are advertised for three to seven weeks, and bids can be submitted and resubmitted any time before the bid opening. Plan backwards from the opening: takeoff in the first third, quotes requested by the middle, bid day reserved for numbers that arrive late.

Electronic bidding

Caltrans major contracts are bid electronically with its bidding software and an online bidding service. First-time bidders need a Bidder ID and a digital ID; Caltrans advises allowing at least 7 days to finish registration. Do it before you need it.

Who does what in a small company

TaskUsually
Bid/no-bid, final priceOwner
Takeoff and labor pricingEstimator or PM
Material and sub quotesEstimator, with the office
Bid forms, bonds, DBE and sub listingOffice, checked by the owner

PM notes

Every estimate becomes the job's budget. Organize it by the same cost codes you'll use to track the job. See Job costing: knowing what each job really made.

Official sources

Caltrans Office Engineer: Bidder's Guide · Electronic bidding.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Registering for electronic bidding the week of the bid.
  • Starting the takeoff after requesting quotes, so suppliers price the wrong quantities.
  • An estimate organized differently from how the job will be tracked.
