Glossary

ABCDFGLNPRST
Awarding body
The public agency that advertises, awards and pays for a public works contract, such as Caltrans, a city or a district. Read more
Bid security
A bid bond, check or cash submitted with a bid, usually at least 10% of the bid, kept by the agency if the winner fails to sign the contract. Read more
Bonding capacity
The single-job and aggregate limits a surety will bond for a contractor, based on its character, capacity and capital. Read more
Certified payrollCertified payroll record (CPR)
Weekly payroll for a public works job, worker by worker, signed under penalty of perjury and filed electronically with DIR. Read more
CSLBContractors State License Board
The California board that licenses contractors and sets license classes. Read more
DASDivision of Apprenticeship Standards
The DIR division that oversees apprenticeship programs and public works apprenticeship requirements. Read more
DAS 140Public Works Contract Award Information
The form a contractor sends to apprenticeship programs to report a public works contract award, before starting work. Read more
DAS 142Request for Dispatch of an Apprentice
The form a contractor uses to ask an apprenticeship program to send apprentices to a public works job. Read more
Davis-Bacon
The federal law requiring locally prevailing wages on federally funded construction; on federal-aid jobs, the higher of the federal and state rate applies. Read more
DBEDisadvantaged Business Enterprise
A certified small business under USDOT's program in 49 CFR Part 26, used on federally funded transportation contracts. Read more
Debarment
A ban from bidding or working on public works for a period, imposed for serious or repeated violations. Read more
DIRDepartment of Industrial Relations
The California department that registers public works contractors, publishes prevailing wage rates and receives certified payroll. Read more
Direct contractor
The contractor that signs the contract directly with the public agency; also called the prime contractor. Read more
FHWA-1273Required Contract Provisions, Federal-Aid Construction Contracts
The federal provisions that must be included in every federal-aid highway contract and subcontract. Read more
Fringe benefits
The part of the prevailing wage paid as health, pension, vacation, training and other benefits, either into plans or as cash. Read more
Good faith efforts
Documented efforts to meet a DBE contract goal when the goal itself isn't met. Read more
Liquidated damages
A fixed amount per day set in the contract; in labor compliance, also an amount equal to unpaid wages owed after an assessment. Read more
Nonresponsive bid
A bid that doesn't follow the bid instructions in a material way and must be rejected. Read more
Notice of completion
A notice, often recorded by the agency, that the work is complete; it starts the deadlines for stop payment notices and bond claims. Read more
Payment bond
A bond, usually 100% of the contract, guaranteeing payment to subcontractors, suppliers and workers; required on public works over $25,000. Read more
Performance bond
A bond, usually 100% of the contract, guaranteeing to the agency that the work will be completed. Read more
Preliminary notice20-day preliminary notice
A notice to the agency, prime and surety that protects a lower-tier claimant's right to a stop payment notice or bond claim. Read more
Prevailing wage
The minimum hourly wage and benefits for each craft and county on public works, published by DIR. Read more
Prevailing wage determination
DIR's published rate sheet for a craft and county, fixed for a job by the bid advertisement date. Read more
Prompt payment
The rules requiring primes and subs to pay their subcontractors within a set time, 7 days under California law, after being paid. Read more
PWC-100
The DIR form an awarding body files to register a public works project, against which certified payroll is submitted. Read more
Responsible bidder
A bidder that has the license, experience, capacity and record to perform the work. Read more
Retention
The part of each progress payment the agency holds until completion, generally capped at 5% on California public works. Read more
Stop payment notice
A written notice to a public agency by an unpaid claimant, requiring it to withhold funds owed to the prime. Read more
Subcontractor listing
The list of subs above one-half of 1% of the bid that a prime must include in a California public works bid. Read more
Substitution
Replacing a listed subcontractor, allowed only with the agency's consent for reasons set in Public Contract Code 4107. Read more
Surety
The company that issues bonds and guarantees the contractor's obligations to the agency and to those it owes. Read more
Training fund contribution
Payments per hour for apprenticeable crafts to an apprenticeship program or the California Apprenticeship Council. Read more

For learning only, not legal advice. Rules change and each agency adds its own: always check the contract, the agency and the current law, and ask a construction attorney when money or a license is at stake.
