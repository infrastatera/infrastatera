Electrical testing

Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: testing proves the wiring is right before it is energized, and the test records prove it to Caltrans.

Common tests

TestWhat it proves
ContinuityEach conductor runs where it should, without breaks
Insulation resistanceInsulation is not damaged; no path to ground
Ground resistanceThe grounding system meets the required value. See Grounding and bonding
Voltage and polarityCorrect supply at each panel and device
Functional testThe system does what it is meant to do

The tests required, how they are done and the pass values are in the specifications and Special Provisions: what they change and why they win.

Test equipment

Multimeters, insulation testers and ground resistance testers. Keep calibration certificates; Caltrans may ask for them. Fiber has its own tests. See Fiber optic communications.

PM notes

Record results in the format of the final test report as you go. See Phase 9: System testing and Caltrans acceptance testing and the punch list.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Energizing circuits before insulation tests.
  • Expired calibration on test equipment.
  • Results that can't be tied to a circuit or location.
