Phase 9: System testing

Phase 9Verify the edition named in your contractNot yet verified against the current edition

In one sentence: phase 9 is your own test run: find and fix the problems before Caltrans comes to watch.

Electrical tests

Voltage, insulation resistance, continuity, grounding, battery operation and solar charging. See Electrical testing.

Communication tests

Fiber performance, router and switch communication, and each device reachable on the network.

Functional tests

Test the system the way Caltrans will use it:

  • Can Caltrans see the camera?
  • Can it reach the switch and the field cabinet?
  • Do the alarms (door open, power fail) reach the TMC?
  • Does the signal, sign or meter do what the plans say?

PM focus

Write every result down in the format of the final test report. A test you can't show didn't happen. Schedule the Caltrans witness tests only after your own tests pass. See Caltrans acceptance testing and the punch list.

Common mistakes / what Caltrans actually checks

  • Inviting Caltrans to witness tests that the crew never ran.
  • Results kept in a notebook instead of the report format.
